## Correctness and pedagogy

- **Section 1 gave away its own answers.** Order IDs encoded the segment (`cad-discount-014`),
  checkout logged `Finished checkout X with payment_rejected`, payment logged the currency, and the
  rejection was the only `WARN` in the stream, so Loki's level histogram printed the count
  outright. Order IDs are now opaque digests, no prose log names an outcome, currency, or discount,
  the rejection sits at `INFO` among 840 lines, and its wording rotates across three phrasings with
  no shared search term. `tests/test_weak_start.py` pins all of this.
- **The headline stat was unstable.** `rate(...[15s])` went `NaN` ten seconds after traffic stopped,
  and grouped traffic meant a window could read 0% or 100%. Traffic is now interleaved so every
  four consecutive requests contain exactly one failure, the burst runs ~20s, and the quiet gap
  exceeds the rate window. Measured readings are 24–27%.
- **The failure-rate panel showed No data during healthy traffic** because the numerator series did
  not exist. `or vector(0)` fixes it.

## Known and accepted

- "Peak business failures" reads 24–27% rather than exactly 25%. It reports the worst 15-second
  window, and the window at the edge of the burst is skewed. Exact counts are in the segment panel
  and in `./lab check metrics`. The guide states the range.
- `_log_weak_rejection` remains in `validation.py` after LAB 2 so participants can compare it with
  the structured event in section 4.
- Tempo's Explore results table renders a `nested` column of raw frame JSON. This is a Grafana 13
  nested-frame artifact, not a lab configuration error.
